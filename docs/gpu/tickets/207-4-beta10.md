## Review (2026-11-02)
Fresh numerics-reviewer recomputed every headline number from raw qref.txt with own code: **all confirmed** (zstd
dumps 352/352 match the manifests). Interpretation **corrected**:
1. The widening is a **LOG effect**: on def the correct band is unchanged (L 0.152 at β = 4 → 0.149 at β = 10); there
   the controls dropped (var8 vs unpermuted host members +0.27/+0.31 → +0.02/+0.08, NS=0 +0.31 → +0.06).
2. On LOG the flag-4 penalty sits on branch-1 points (+0.39…+0.48 at β = 10, branch 2 −0.12…0.00), consistent with
   Householder QR on row-reordered graded matrices (not proved; a CPU-only row-sort test could). It is not a
   tool defect (unpermute exact, branch-confined). **var8 and TAUSWAP=8 are still ≈ 0.2 decades worse than the
   unpermuted host members** (L 0.139; var8 +0.217, TAUSWAP +0.202; device −0.037). So a LOG β = 10 bound near 0.28
   admits a device ≈ 1.6× less accurate than the host ordering — a stated cost, not "no defect".
